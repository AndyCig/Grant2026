*N.B.: **Questions and comments in blue may be overwritten or deleted**.
One document must be uploaded imperatively as an unprotected PDF
file (document generated from a word processor file to a PDF, no scanned
document). Please stick to the following formatting requirements: font
size: 10 pt, line spacing: 1.15. Both formatting requirements as well as
the maximum number of pages are **mandatory and must be respected**,
otherwise the pre-proposal will be declared not eligible. Such
requirements help the Expert Panel members to read and compare the
pre-proposals.*

***The overall page limit for sections 3 – 6 is 13 pages. All sections
that are included in the template must be included in the finished
proposal; do not delete sections** (blue info text can of course be
deleted). **Proposals which exceed the page limit will be declared
ineligible.***

***Please note that attachments are not allowed, with the exceptions of
LOIs, which can be added to the finished PDF as an annex that will not
count towards the page limit.***

2.  **Abstract (4000 characters max.)**

*\[This exact same information will also be inserted in the online
submission system at
[uefiscdi-direct.ro](https://uefiscdi-direct.ro/)\]*

*Short presentation of project content and goals. The abstract should

3.  **Quality of work, project objectives and targets (rec. 3 pages)**

    1.  **Objectives and targets, background and positioning with
        respect to the state-of the-art**

*Give a detailed description of the project goals and planned results
based on the description of the initial situation and the societal,
social, economic, environmental or technological problems and challenges
related to the call topic(s) and to be solved in the project.*

*Point out the innovative aspect in your approach, taking into account
the state of the art/knowledge (improvements from existing
solutions/conditions, originality of approach, degree of novelty,
technological leap). Indicate how it is positioned with respect to the
state-of-the art and in particular, how it built on and complement
existing knowledge and experiences as achieved in earlier relevant
European, national and regional research and innovation (R&I)
programmes, pilots and test implementations and field labs.*

*Describe the conceptual (scientific and/or technical) approaches you
intend to use to achieve the defined goals and expected results.*

*Explain in few words how your project responds to the Do no significant
harm principle (DNSH)*[^2]*.*

2.  **Relevance and contribution of the project to the goals of the
    call**

*Indicate how and to what extent the project addresses the chosen call
topic(s). Furthermore, describe how the project contributes to the
objectives of the call.*

3.  **Overall project type (research-oriented or innovation-oriented
    approach)**

*This call welcomes a broad range of research and innovation. Explain
the main positioning and objectives of the project (production of new
knowledge, development of a technology, implementation and test of a
solution…). Justify why you choose the research-oriented approach or the
innovation-oriented approach.*

4.  **Added value of international co-operation**

*Describe the added value of the planned transnational co-operation with
regard to the objectives of your project.*

4.  **Expected outcome and impact (rec. 3 pages)**

    1.  **Impact of the project to the communities**

*Describe the extent to which the project is likely to be a value to
potential user communities (e.g. public sector, urban government
authorities, private sector, civil society, research organisations).*

*When relevant (mainly for the innovation-oriented approaches), indicate
how you estimate the potential market for your project’s results.*

2.  **Knowledge Hub activities and dissemination (minimum 1 page)**

*Briefly describe how you envisage to disseminate your project’s results
and outputs toward end users and practitioners. Explain what will be the
contribution of your project’s results to the DUT Knowledge Hub (refer
to section 5.2.3 of the Call text) and what long term impact are
expected into the communities. Please designate one expert representing
your project and involved in the Knowledge Hub activities.*

5.  **Quality and efficiency of project implementation (rec. 5 pages)**

    1.  **Outline of the work programme**

*Give an outline of the envisioned work programme, the results expected
within the duration of the project and the main milestones.*

2.  **Project consortium, transdisciplinary, stakeholder’s involvement**

*Describe how your project addresses, as a consortium, all scientific,
technical and organisational competences required to implement the
project.*

*Explain the contributions and complementarity of the scientific
disciplines and of the partners involved and explain how the project
addressed the questions of interdisciplinarity, transdisciplinary or
cross sectoral collaboration.*

*All projects are expected to clearly engage relevant stakeholders,
including the public sector, private sector, civil society throughout
the duration of projects. Explain their role in the project.*

3.  **Use of AI in Proposal Preparation**

*When preparing a Proposal, Project Consortia are fully responsible for
its content, including any parts generated with the support of AI tools
and must be transparent in disclosing this. Use this section to provide
a list of sources used to generate content and citations as well as to
describe which AI tools were used and how they were utilized. See the
Call Text (section 3.3 Preparing And Submitting An Application) for more
information.*

*Indicate at least 5 relevant references related to the consortium
(scientific publications, previous projects, practical achievements…)
for this project.*
