# DUT Call 2026: Pre-Proposal Template Summary

Sources: [`call-docs/DUT_Call_2026_Preproposal_Template.docx`](call-docs/DUT_Call_2026_Preproposal_Template.docx) and
[`call-docs/DUT_Call_2026_Submission_Guidelines.pdf`](call-docs/DUT_Call_2026_Submission_Guidelines.pdf).
Text versions are in `call-docs/` (`.md` / `.txt`).

## Key dates
| Stage | Deadline |
|---|---|
| **Pre-proposal** | **17 November 2026, 13:00 CET** |
| Full proposal (invited only) | 15 April 2027, 13:00 CET |

## Hard rules (breaking them makes the proposal ineligible)
- Submit **one unprotected PDF** made from a word processor (not a scan), uploaded in UDiManager → *General Information → 1. Pre-proposal Info*.
- **Font size 10 pt, line spacing 1.15.**
- **Sections 3–6 together: 13 pages max.**
- **Keep every section** in the template. You can delete the blue guidance text.
- **No attachments**, except Letters of Intent (LOIs), which can go in an annex that doesn't count toward the page limit.
- Use only **one** R&I approach (ROA *or* IOA). **Cross-cutting topics (topic 1 in each pathway) are for IOA only.**
- The abstract must be **identical** in the PDF and in the online system.

## Template structure

### 1. Project overview (table; also entered in UDiManager)
| Field | Constraint |
|---|---|
| Short title / acronym | ≤ 20 characters incl. spaces |
| Full title | ≤ 150 characters incl. spaces |
| Project coordinator | Organisation + PI name |
| Main R&I approach | Tick one: **ROA** (research-oriented) or **IOA** (innovation-oriented) |
| Main transition pathway | Tick one: **15mC** (15-minute City), **CUE** (Circular Urban Economies), **PED** (Positive Energy Districts) |
| Call topics | Tick all that apply. Topics from secondary pathways are allowed (see list below) |
| Keywords | Up to 5 |
| Total project costs / requested funds (EUR) | Calculated automatically from the online budget |
| Duration | **≤ 36 months**, with expected start and end (MM.YYYY) |
| Total effort | Person-months (calculated automatically online) |

**Call topics**
| Pathway | Topic 1 (cross-cutting, **IOA only**) | Topic 2 | Topic 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **15mC** | Adaptive reuse of existing urban structures and spaces | Align ambitions for mobility transitions across sectors | Nurture neighbourhood economies: activate innovation in local everyday offers |
| **CUE** | Adaptive reuse of existing urban structures and spaces | Innovative maintenance of urban green and blue spaces | Rethinking urban production for circular and resilient cities |

### 2. Abstract (≤ 4000 characters)
Project content and goals. Must match the abstract in the submission system word for word.

### 3. Quality of work, objectives and targets (recommended ~3 pages)
- **3.1 Objectives, targets, background and state of the art:** goals and planned results, starting from the current situation and the societal, economic, environmental or technological problem. Explain what is innovative compared with the state of the art and how the project builds on earlier EU, national and regional R&I work (pilots, field labs). Describe the scientific or technical approach. Briefly say how the project complies with **Do No Significant Harm (DNSH)**.
- **3.2 Relevance to the call:** how the project addresses the chosen topic(s) and the call's objectives.
- **3.3 Project type (ROA vs IOA):** main positioning (new knowledge, technology development, testing a solution, etc.) and **why** you chose that approach.
- **3.4 Added value of international cooperation:** why the work needs to be transnational.

### 4. Expected outcome and impact (recommended ~3 pages)
- **4.1 Impact on communities:** value to users such as the public sector, urban authorities, the private sector, civil society and research. For IOA, estimate the potential market.
- **4.2 Knowledge Hub activities and dissemination (at least 1 page, the only section with a minimum):** how results reach end users and practitioners, what the project contributes to the **DUT Knowledge Hub** (Call Text §5.2.3), and long-term impact. **Name one expert** to represent the project in Knowledge Hub activities.

### 5. Quality and efficiency of implementation (recommended ~5 pages)
- **5.1 Work programme outline:** work programme, expected results and main milestones.
- **5.2 Consortium, transdisciplinarity and stakeholder involvement:** competences covered, how disciplines and partners complement each other, and inter-/transdisciplinary or cross-sector collaboration. Describe the role of stakeholders (public, private, civil society) **throughout** the project.
- **5.3 Use of AI in proposal preparation (new):** the consortium is fully responsible for the content. List the sources used, and say which AI tools were used and how (Call Text §3.3).

### 6. References (recommended ~2 pages)
- **6.1 Consortium references (~1 page):** at least 5 relevant items (publications, previous projects, practical achievements).
- **6.2 Scientific references (~1 page):** external state-of-the-art literature.

**Page budget:** 3 + 3 + 5 + 2 = 13 recommended pages for sections 3–6, which is exactly the hard cap. Sections 1–2 aren't counted toward it.

## Entered online, not in the PDF (UDiManager, from the guidelines)
- **Consortium:** at least 3 eligible applicants from 3 participating countries. It must include **at least one urban government authority** (as Main Applicant, Co-applicant or Cooperation Partner). Cross-cutting-topic proposals also need **at least one funded private-sector partner**.
- A **PIC** is required for every partner, including Cooperation Partners. Validation isn't required.
- Every partner must **confirm by e-mail** through the system invitation (it can be resent once).
- **Principal Contact Point:** in at most 2 proposals, and PI of the Main Applicant in at most 1. Otherwise all the proposals involved are ineligible.
- Researchers: research category and ORCID. Each partner's budget is entered under *Financial Information*. The totals in section 1 are calculated from this.
- Cooperation Partners: their budget can be 0 or in-kind. An LOI is needed only at the full-proposal stage.
- New option: consent to publish the abstract for the "widening" procedure (Call Text §4.1).
- Check each national funding agency's own rules (Annex A) before submitting.
- *Verify* can be run any number of times. **Finalize** is final, so press it only when the proposal is complete.
